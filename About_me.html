Who is Lucia Idali Arras?

Throughout my career, I have worked in clinical, educational, and organizational settings, providing therapeutic care, teaching university courses, and developing training programs for companies.

These experiences have allowed me to understand the complexity of the contexts in which people develop and the importance of creating safe spaces where they can explore their experiences with clarity and confidence.

Through brief therapy, I collaborate with each client to identify specific goals and move toward solutions that generate significant changes within a timeframe appropriate to their needs.

My systemic approach allows me to observe the family, social, and work dynamics that influence their well-being, while the Ericksonian perspective guides creative, respectful, and strategic interventions that honor the uniqueness of each individual's story.

My commitment is to offer professional support that combines clinical focus, human sensitivity, and a deep conviction in people's ability to transform themselves and build more fulfilling lives.
