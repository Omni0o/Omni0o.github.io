# PSIC Lucia Idali Arras Website
I am currently making a website for my mom. She is a phsycologist that wants to spread out and advertize online. 
The website is to answer questions of who she is and can serve as an introduction.

The website can also help explain how she works to potential new clients to see if she is the right therapist for you. 
The website will contain a spanish version because she can give therapy in spanish and english.

The website can also serve as a place where people can place an appointment to see her.

The website contains 5 html pages that are connected through the navigation bar. It was made with the purpose to see it from a laptop or phone. To see the phone view you will have to make the page smaller on a laptop or visit the website from a phone.

Navigation Bar and Footer
  1. Hovering over the Title makes the text translucent.
  2. Hovering over the burger menu icon pops up a hidden navigation bar.
  3. Clicking the text on the hidden navigation bar allows you to be taken to the other html pages.
  4. Hovering over the text in the hidden navigation bar plays an animation where the text grows and gets underlined.
  5. The footer contain the tittle that when hovered over will make the text translucent.
  6. The footer also holds the privacy, and terms of service pages.
  7. hovering over privacy and terms of service plays an animation that underlines the text.
